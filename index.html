Connecting Your iOS Shortcut or Database Export

This app runs entirely in your browser and supports three ways to swap in your latest workout data:

  1. Direct File Upload / Drag & Drop: Tap Upload .gkdb / .csv and pick your latest GymKing .gkdb or .csv (or Lyfta .csv) export from the Files app.
  2. Automated URL Sync via iOS Shortcuts: Host your latest export at a public https:// link (e.g., GitHub raw or Dropbox direct link) and open this app with ?data=YOUR_HTTPS_URL, or paste the URL into the box above and tap Load URL.
  3. Clipboard / Share Sheet: In your iOS Shortcut, copy the latest CSV/JSON text to the clipboard, tap Paste Data, and paste it in.

Your most recently loaded dataset is saved locally in your browser so it persists across reloads.
