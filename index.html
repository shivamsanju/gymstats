iOS Sync & Data Connection

This dashboard runs locally in your browser. Update data securely through three native-feeling methods:

  1. Direct Upload: Tap the file icon in the top right to select your .gkdb or .csv from iOS Files.
  2. Shortcuts Automation (Pro): Host your export on iCloud Drive/Dropbox. Create a shortcut that grabs the file content and opens Safari with a Data URI, or pass a public URL via ?data=URL.
  3. Clipboard Sync: Copy your JSON/CSV text on your phone, tap the paste icon, and load it instantly.

Your dataset persists in local memory during your session.
